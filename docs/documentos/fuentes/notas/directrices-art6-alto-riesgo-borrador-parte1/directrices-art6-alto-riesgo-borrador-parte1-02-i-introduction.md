I.

Introduction
(1) The Artificial Intelligence Act (the ‘AI Act’)1, which entered into force on 1 August 2024, lays down harmonised rules for the placing on the market, putting into service, and use of artificial intelligence (‘AI’) in the Union2. That regulation aims to promote innovation in and the uptake of
AI, while ensuring a high level of protection of health, safety, and fundamental rights in the Union, including democracy and the rule of law. The AI Act follows a risk-based approach, classifying AI systems into different risk categories depending on the risks they pose to health, safety and fundamental rights.
